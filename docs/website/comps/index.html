One record, from first report to fix.

Every step is written to the same history, so a resident, an officer and an administrator are always looking at the same facts.

  1. 1

    Report

    Photos, location and a short description, from the web or the Android app, even offline.

    REPORTEDMon 09:14CL-2481

  2. 2

    Triage

    AI suggests a category, severity and department, and flags likely duplicates.

    TRIAGEDMon 09:14Roads / High (suggested)

  3. 3

    Assign

    Staff confirm or override the suggestion, then assign the task with a deadline.

    ASSIGNEDMon 09:31Due Fri 18:00

  4. 4

    Fix

    The officer acknowledges, starts work and adds updates from the field.

    IN PROGRESSWed 08:40

  5. 5

    Evidence

    Before and after photos, notes, materials and duration are attached to the closure.

    FIXEDWed 11:054 photos · 2 h 10 m

  6. 6

    Feedback

    The resident rates the fix, or appeals if it was not resolved.

    CLOSEDWed 16:20Rated by resident

What it does

  • Reads a report's title and description
  • Suggests a category and a severity
  • Groups similar reports to flag duplicates
  • Suggests the responsible department
  • Flags low-confidence results for review

What it does not do

  • Look at photos
  • Make the final decision
  • Learn from your reports; models are used as published
  • Guarantee accuracy

CivicLens is an independent demonstration product built by Tejas Bhor. It is not a government service.
