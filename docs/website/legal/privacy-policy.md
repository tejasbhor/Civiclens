# Privacy policy

*Last updated: [DECIDE: publication date]*

CivicLens is an independent demonstration product built by Tejas Bhor. It is not a government service. This policy
explains what personal data the demo handles, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and how to exercise your rights.
It is designed with the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind. It is not a claim of
legal compliance or certification.

## 1. Please use sample data only

CivicLens is a demonstration. Do not submit real complaints, real emergencies or sensitive personal information. Demo data
may be reset or deleted at any time. If you have an emergency, contact your local emergency services (in India, dial 112).

## 3. What we collect and why

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **Account details:** phone number, email address, name, profile photo, language, notification choices | Sign you in, verify ownership, contact you, personalize the app |
| **Staff sign-in data:** hashed password, two-factor secret, department, employee ID | Authenticate officers and administrators |
| **Reports:** title, description, category, severity, status history | Handle the issue you report |
| **Location of the issue:** coordinates, address, landmark, ward, district, state, pincode | Send the report to the right place |
| **Photos** (and audio, if your version supports it `[VERIFY C12]`) | Show the issue and the fix |
| **Officer location while on duty** | Coordinate field work `[VERIFY]` |
| **AI results:** suggested category, confidence, duplicate grouping | Triage reports |
| **Notifications and device push token** | Send status updates you have enabled |
| **Security data:** IP address, browser or device details, session information, action logs | Keep accounts secure and keep an audit trail |
| **Error reports** (only if enabled `[VERIFY Q-04]`) | Find and fix bugs |

We do **not** collect Aadhaar numbers, DigiLocker data, payment details or advertising identifiers.

The marketing website (this site) sets no advertising or tracking cookies. If you choose to allow optional analytics, it counts
page views without cookies or personal profiles (see the Cookie policy).

## 4. Photos and location

Photos are re-processed when uploaded, which removes embedded camera metadata `[VERIFY C11 before keeping this sentence]`.
The coordinates you provide with a report are kept so the issue can be located. Reports may be visible to staff of the
responsible department; whether a report is publicly visible depends on the report's visibility setting `[VERIFY C13]`.

## 5. Who receives your data

| Recipient | What for |
|---|---|
| Staff and departments using the platform | Handle the report you submit |
| Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (hosting) `[DECIDE Q-03: region]` | Store and run the service |
| Email delivery provider `[VERIFY Q-04]` | Send verification and notification emails |

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us to:
- **Access:** tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy;
- **Correct:** fix inaccurate data;
- **Erase:** delete your account and data, subject to keeping anonymized workflow records that protect the integrity of the audit trail;
- **Withdraw consent:** stop optional processing such as analytics or notifications;
- **Nominate:** name another person to exercise your rights if you cannot;
- **Complain:** use the grievance route below.

Email `[DECIDE Q-01]` from the phone or email registered on your account. There is no self-service export or deletion yet.
We aim to reply within `[DECIDE: e.g. 30 days]`. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Data Protection Board of India
once its complaint process applies to you.

## 9. Children

CivicLens is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has
given us data, email us and we will delete it.
