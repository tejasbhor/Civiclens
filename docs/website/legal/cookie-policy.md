# Cookie policy

*Last updated: [DECIDE: publication date]*

This page lists what the CivicLens marketing site and web app store in your browser, why, and how to control it.
Cookies are small files; "browser storage" (localStorage) works the same way for this purpose.

## 1. Marketing site (civiclens.space)

| Name | Type | Purpose | Lasts | Set |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `civiclens_consent` | Essential | Remembers your cookie choices | 12 months | When you make a choice |
| `theme` | Preference | Remembers light or dark mode | Until cleared | When you change theme |
| Analytics cookie or ID | Optional | Counts page views without profiling `[DECIDE T-03: vendor, or remove this row]` | `[DECIDE]` | Only if you allow Analytics |

Nothing else is set. No advertising, tracking or social-media cookies. No third-party requests are made before you choose.
`[VERIFY in devtools before publishing]`

## 2. Web app (app.civiclens.space)

The app uses browser storage, not tracking cookies:

| Key | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `access_token`, `refresh_token` | Keep you signed in |
| `user`, `remember_me` | Remember your profile and sign-in choice |
| `theme` | Remember light or dark mode |
| `civiclens_cookie_consent` | Remember your choice |

These are essential for the app to work. They are removed when you sign out or clear your browser data.
`[VERIFY: list matches code at publication; update when tokens move to cookies (F3)]`

## 3. Your choices

- Use "Cookie preferences" in the footer at any time to change or withdraw your choice.
- Rejecting optional analytics never limits access to the site.
- You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.

## 4. Categories

| Category | Consent needed | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | No | Always on |
| Preference | No, it stores only what you chose | On when you use the feature |
| Analytics | Yes | Off |
